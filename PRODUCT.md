# Rabuan ALP · Pertamina Patra Niaga

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## What it is
A browser-based presentation deck for the "Rabuan" session of the P3MD Action Learning Project (ALP). Four participant groups (Kel 13, 9, 11, 12) present one joint story about their Business Immersion at PT Pertamina Patra Niaga, Integrated Terminal Jakarta (Plumpang), held 7 October 2026.

## Audience and scene
Mentors (Pertamina practitioners), academic coaches (SBM ITB) and fellow participants, in a meeting room with a projector or large screen, lights on. One presenter drives with a clicker or arrow keys; each group presents its own section.

## Job
Open with a question that pulls the room in (one tank truck: one product or several?), then walk A → D: company and business model, field process, challenges and root causes, transformation and action plan.

## Positioning
One joint story by four groups (Kel 13, 9, 11, 12) from the same visit: every number, photo and quote is sourced, and numbers that differ between groups are shown side by side.

## Operating Context
- Presented once, live, at the Rabuan session of the ALP: one presenter per section drives it with a clicker or arrow keys, one click per build.
- Afterwards shared as a PDF (to mentors and coaches, or on the ALP Drive); the PDF shows every slide fully built.

## Brand commitments (pinned by the user)
- Use the Pertamina design system as found on the internet: official logo files, logo colours red/blue/green, Rethink Sans (pertaminapatraniaga.com), Tabler icons (pertamina.com).
- Every fact, quote and image comes from a real, citable source: participants' own BI photos, Wikimedia Commons, group materials on Google Drive, named news articles. Nothing invented. Sources are listed in SOURCES.md and on the last slide.
- v2 (10 Nov 2026): visual style follows the PT Semen Indonesia Business Immersion deck (dense corporate slides), in Pertamina Patra Niaga identity. Output is HTML deck + PDF. No 3D.

## Constraints
- 1920×1080 stage scaled to the screen; works offline from local files (no CDN at show time).
- Section B is a draft built from Kel 9's individual BI reports (S01–S09) plus Kel 11–13 material until Kel 9 uploads its deck.
- Where groups disagree on numbers, show each with its source; do not harmonise silently.

## Evidence on Hand
- Participants' BI photos: `assets/photos/`, originals with descriptions in `../Video/research_notes/business-immersion/originals` and `annotations.json`.
- Group materials in the Drive folder `Rabuan` (ALP folder); individual BI reports S01–S39.
- Labelled Codex illustrations in `assets/illus/` for concepts no photo shows (see SOURCES.md).
- Missing: Kel 9's official deck; section B stays marked as a draft until it arrives. Do not fabricate figures or quotes to fill gaps.

## Product Principles
1. Sourced or not shown.
2. Field facts first, interpretation after.
3. Four groups, one route A → D.
4. A draft is marked as a draft.
5. Illustrations are always labelled; photos are always real.
