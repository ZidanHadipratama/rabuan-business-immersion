Scope: Rabuan ALP deck (index.html), visitor mode Experience (stage presentation, presenter-driven).
Audience: mentors, coaches, participants in a lit meeting room with a projector. Job: open with the tank-truck question, then A to D.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The deck is one fuel run from terminal to customer; the tank truck's sealed compartments are the organising idea (four groups, one truck). It refuses the corporate template of title, bullet list and stock icon per slide.
OWN-WORLD: Pertamina Patra Niaga terminal at midday. Ground #F8FAFC and ink #0F172A for working slides; navy #05416D fields for photo and 3D moments. Red #EA1E30 for the one figure per slide that matters, blue #086DB5, green #AAC237 as section markers. Fuel dye colours (Pertalite green, Pertamax blue, Turbo red) only inside the tank. Rethink Sans, JetBrains Mono for measured numbers. Thin steel rules, hazard stripe used once as a section edge, Tabler outline icons.
STORY: The room guesses, sees the answer inside a real truck, then follows the run: know the company, see the field, find the problem, change it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed BI photo DSCF2614 of the 24.000 l truck crossing the terminal, slow push in; question set in two lines at left on the open asphalt; two answer options bottom-left, 10 s countdown bottom-right.
FORM: Pinned whole by the user brief (Pertamina design system; opening tank-truck question with the answer on the next slide; real references only).
