# Rabuan ALP · Pertamina Patra Niaga

## What it is
A browser-based presentation deck for the "Rabuan" session of the P3MD Action Learning Project (ALP). Four participant groups (Kel 13, 9, 11, 12) present one joint story about their Business Immersion at PT Pertamina Patra Niaga, Integrated Terminal Jakarta (Plumpang), held 7 October 2026.

## Audience and scene
Mentors (Pertamina practitioners), academic coaches (SBM ITB) and fellow participants, in a meeting room with a projector or large screen, lights on. One presenter drives with a clicker or arrow keys; each group presents its own section.

## Job
Open with a question that pulls the room in (one tank truck: one product or several?), then walk A → D: company and business model, field process, challenges and root causes, transformation and action plan.

## Brand commitments (pinned by the user)
- Use the Pertamina design system as found on the internet: official logo files, logo colours red/blue/green, Rethink Sans (pertaminapatraniaga.com), Tabler icons (pertamina.com).
- Every fact, quote and image comes from a real, citable source: participants' own BI photos, Wikimedia Commons, group materials on Google Drive, named news articles. Nothing invented. Sources are listed in SOURCES.md and on the last slide.
- Lots of animation, visuals and 3D.

## Constraints
- 1920×1080 stage scaled to the screen; works offline from local files (no CDN at show time).
- Section B is empty until Kel 9 uploads its material.
- Where groups disagree on numbers, show each with its source; do not harmonise silently.

Assumptions (inferred from the brief, not interviewed): light room projector; Indonesian copy; presenter uses arrow keys or clicker.
